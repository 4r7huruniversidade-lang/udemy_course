Program Overview
In this course, we will cover everything from the ground up. Here’s a glimpse of what you can expect:

Foundational Skills: Begin with Python programming, ensuring you have a solid base to build upon.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Learn techniques to visualize and interpret data, making informed decisions based on your findings.

Statistics: Gain a strong understanding of statistical concepts that are vital for data analysis and model evaluation.

SQL: Master the art of querying databases to extract and manipulate data effectively.

Machine Learning (ML): Dive into core ML algorithms and their applications, from supervised to unsupervised learning.

Deep Learning (DL): Explore neural networks and their powerful capabilities in various domains.

Natural Language Processing (NLP): Understand the intricacies of processing and analyzing human language data.

Time Series Analysis: Learn how to analyze time-dependent data and forecast future trends.

Transformers & Generative AI: Discover cutting-edge techniques in AI, including transformer models and their applications.

Model Deployment: Learn how to deploy some of your machine learning or deep learning models on the Cloud.
